# DynaMac — Product truth

## What it is
Native macOS menu bar / notch HUD by Surface Tension.

## Price
$2.99 one-time. No free tier. No subscription.

## Tabs (marketing names)
1. **Now Playing** — music/media HUD + live agents panel
2. **Intent** — calendar, to-dos, focus timer
3. **Shelf** — file holding, drag-and-drop, AirDrop

## Shipped features (market these only)
- Music controls (Spotify, Apple Music, etc.) in the notch
- Live agents panel: shows Cursor, Claude, Codex window titles when Accessibility is on; tap Open
- Intent tab: calendar scopes (today/week/month), local to-dos, focus timer
- Shelf: hold files in notch, share via AirDrop
- Liquid glass / dark mode notch chrome

## Do NOT market
- Soen, Glance, Look, Listen, local-first AI, screen read, voice, Ollama
- Free tier or Pro vs Free comparison
- Fake metrics or testimonials

## Platform
macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon & Intel.
